A LITTLE MATH UNDER THE WOOD

Beautifully simple.
Precisely checked.

The scene record and its checker run in Lean 4.35.0. The pretty pixels are not the evidence.

Place without overlap.

A legal drop preserves disjoint interiors, unique IDs, and inventory. Its whole straight-down insertion path is clear.

Account for every load.

Accepted certificates use nonnegative contact forces and exact integer arithmetic. Each plank balances vertical force and both first moments.

What this does not prove

Real-world safety, friction, bending, sideways stability, resistance to a bump, or Rapier's moving simulation. The certificate solver can miss a valid witness; “not certified” does not mean “impossible.”
